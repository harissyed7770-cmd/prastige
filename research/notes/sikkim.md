# Research Notes — Sikkim

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (Sikkim tourism guides, permit info) +
established geographic facts.

## Positioning

The Eastern Himalaya done accessibly: Gangtok as a comfortable 1,650 m base,
day trips climbing to a sacred glacial lake (Tsomgo, 3,753 m) and one of the
world's highest motorable passes (Nathula, 4,310 m), with Kanchenjunga — the
world's third-highest mountain — on the horizon. Audience: adventure seekers,
but the adventure is altitude-and-permit flavoured, not technical.

## Best time

- **March–May** for rhododendron bloom and clear spring air; **October–
  December** for the most reliable Kanchenjunga views. Winter buries Tsomgo
  and Nathula in snow (a draw for snow-seekers); monsoon (Jun–Sep) means
  landslide-prone roads.

## Permits (important, checkable)

- Tsomgo Lake and Nathula Pass sit in a protected border zone — Indian
  nationals need a Protected Area Permit arranged through registered
  operators; **Nathula is closed to visitors on Mondays and Tuesdays**.
  Foreign nationals cannot visit Nathula.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Tsomgo (Changu) Lake** — glacial, oval, at 3,753 m about 40 km from
   Gangtok; sacred locally; partially frozen in winter; yak rides at the
   shore.
2. **Nathula Pass** — 4,310 m on the old Lhasa trade route at the
   India–China border, ~56 km from Gangtok; among the world's highest
   motorable passes; permit-only, closed Mon–Tue.
3. **Rumtek Monastery** — Sikkim's largest monastery, ~23 km from Gangtok;
   seat-in-exile of the Karmapa (Kagyu school of Tibetan Buddhism).
4. **Kanchenjunga (8,586 m)** — world's third-highest peak; best seen
   Oct–Dec from Gangtok viewpoints (Tashi Viewpoint) and Pelling.
5. **MG Marg, Gangtok** — the capital's pedestrian-only, traffic-free
   promenade of cafes and shops.
6. **Gangtok Ropeway** — cable car over the capital with valley and ridge
   views.

## Activities shortlist

- Day drive Gangtok → Tsomgo Lake → Baba Mandir → Nathula (permit)
- Sunrise Kanchenjunga view from Tashi Viewpoint
- Butter-lamp morning prayers at Rumtek
- Gangtok Ropeway ride
- River rafting on the Teesta (grade II–III stretches)
- Cafe crawl on pedestrian MG Marg; momos + thukpa food trail
- Rhododendron/alpine flower walks in spring
- Optional extension: Pelling skywalk / West Sikkim

## Sample itinerary (6 days — matches Sikkim Himalayan Adventure 5N/6D)

D1 arrive Bagdogra/NJP, drive to Gangtok. D2 Gangtok: Rumtek, ropeway,
MG Marg. D3 Tsomgo Lake + Baba Mandir + Nathula (permit day). D4 Teesta
rafting + Tashi Viewpoint sunrise. D5 day trip toward Ravangla/Temi tea
garden. D6 depart.

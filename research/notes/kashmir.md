# Research Notes — Kashmir

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (Dal Lake / Gulmarg / Pahalgam
guides) + established geographic facts.

## Positioning

The classic "paradise valley" trio: Srinagar (houseboats and Mughal gardens on
Dal Lake), Gulmarg (one of the world's highest cable cars; winter skiing) and
Pahalgam (pine-valley walks on the Lidder). Two completely different seasons
sell it: spring/summer meadows and winter snow.

## Best time

- **April–June**: gardens in bloom, shikara season on Dal Lake, Pahalgam
  walks. **Late December–February**: snow season — Gulmarg becomes India's
  premier ski resort. Autumn (Oct–Nov) brings chinar trees turning red-gold
  and saffron harvest at Pampore.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Dal Lake, Srinagar** — famous for cedar houseboats, shikara taxis,
   floating vegetable gardens and a floating market; shikaras typically seat
   4–6.
2. **Gulmarg Gondola** — among the world's highest cable cars; two stages,
   Gulmarg → Kongdoori → Apharwat, reaching roughly 3,950 m near the summit
   ridge.
3. **Mughal Gardens, Srinagar** — Nishat Bagh (1633) and Shalimar Bagh
   (1619, laid out under Emperor Jahangir) terrace down toward Dal Lake.
4. **Pahalgam** — "Valley of Shepherds" at the Lidder River; gateway to
   Betaab Valley and Aru; pony trails through pine forest.
5. **Sonamarg** — "Meadow of Gold" at ~2,700 m; summer base for the
   Thajiwas Glacier walk.
6. **Saffron fields of Pampore** — among the world's few saffron-growing
   regions; purple crocus bloom late Oct–early Nov.

## Activities shortlist

- Overnight stay on a Dal Lake houseboat
- Dawn shikara ride to the floating vegetable market
- Gulmarg Gondola both stages; skiing/snowboarding in winter
- Pony trek from Pahalgam into Betaab or Aru valley
- Mughal gardens circuit (Nishat, Shalimar, Chashme Shahi)
- Wazwan tasting (rista, rogan josh, gushtaba) + kahwa tea
- Autumn chinar walk + Pampore saffron fields
- Thajiwas Glacier walk from Sonamarg (summer)

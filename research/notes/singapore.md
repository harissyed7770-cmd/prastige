# Research Notes — Singapore

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (attraction guides, Indian-traveller
visa guides) + established facts.

## Positioning

The polished city-break: 5–6 h direct from Bengaluru, family gold (Universal
Studios, Night Safari, aquarium) plus couple-friendly spectacle (Supertree
light show, Marina Bay skyline). Compact — 4–5 days covers the icons. Big
Indian-community comfort factor (Little India, vegetarian food easy).

## Best time

- **Year-round destination**; November–March pleasant/festive, February–April
  has the least rain. Equatorial: brief daily showers possible anytime.

## Visa (checkable, for Indian travellers — researched 2026)

- Ordinary Indian passports need a visa before travel; e-visa via authorized
  agents, typically 3–5 working days. Direct flights from Bengaluru and five
  other metros. Re-verify at booking.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Gardens by the Bay** — 101-hectare garden complex; Supertrees 25–50 m
   tall with the free nightly Garden Rhapsody show; Cloud Forest dome holds
   the world's tallest indoor waterfall (~35 m); Flower Dome is among the
   world's largest glass greenhouses.
2. **Marina Bay Sands SkyPark** — observation deck across the triple towers;
   free Spectra light-and-water show at the promenade nightly (8 & 9 pm,
   extra Fri/Sat slot).
3. **Sentosa** — resort island by cable car/monorail; Universal Studios
   Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium among the world's largest.
4. **Singapore Zoo & Night Safari** — the Night Safari was the world's
   first nocturnal wildlife park (1994).
5. **Hawker centres** — UNESCO intangible-heritage-listed hawker culture
   (2020); chicken rice, laksa, satay at Maxwell/Lau Pa Sat.
6. **Little India & Chinatown** — Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Buddha Tooth
   Relic Temple; heritage shophouse streets.
7. **Jewel Changi** — the Rain Vortex, world's tallest indoor waterfall in
   an airport (~40 m), inside the mall at Changi.

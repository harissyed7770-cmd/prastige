# Research Notes — France

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (season guides, Schengen visa info
for Indian nationals) + established facts.

## Positioning

The bucket-list Europe trip: Paris (Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Versailles) plus the
Mediterranean glamour of the Riviera (Nice, Monaco). Our package pairs both —
7N/8D "Paris & French Riviera". Sell the contrast: museums-and-boulevards
north, beach-promenade-and-corniche south, connected by a fast TGV run.

## Best time

- **April–May and September–October**: mild, fewer queues, shoulder pricing;
  the Riviera is mild nearly year-round. March has notably shorter lines at
  the Eiffel Tower/Louvre/Versailles. Nice Carnival (Feb) draws ~1M visitors.

## Visa (checkable, for Indian travellers)

- Schengen visa required; €90 adult / €45 child 6–12 / free under 6; up to
  90 days, valid across the Schengen area; biometrics at VFS on first
  application (repeat applicants within 59 months can apply without new
  biometrics). Flag: re-verify at booking, rules change.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Eiffel Tower** — built 1889 for the Exposition Universelle by Gustave
   Eiffel's company; ~330 m with antennas; summit lifts.
2. **Louvre** — world's most-visited museum; glass pyramid entrance (1989,
   I. M. Pei); home of the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo.
3. **Palace of Versailles** — Louis XIV's palace ~20 km from Paris; Hall of
   Mirrors (73 m, 357 mirrors); UNESCO site.
4. **Seine river cruise** — UNESCO-listed riverbanks; Notre-Dame (reopened
   Dec 2024 after the 2019 fire) seen from the water.
5. **Nice & Promenade des Anglais** — 7 km seafront promenade; old-town
   flower market at Cours Saleya; Nice's winter-resort townscape is itself
   UNESCO-listed (2021).
6. **Monaco & Monte-Carlo** — second-smallest country in the world (~2 km²);
   Casino de Monte-Carlo (1863); F1 street circuit corners walkable.
7. **Èze village** — medieval perched village ~430 m above the sea between
   Nice and Monaco, exotic garden at the summit.

# Research Notes — Thailand

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (season guides, Thailand visa rules
for Indians 2026) + established facts.

## Positioning

India's easiest big international holiday: visa-free for Indians (60 days),
short direct flights, and a two-act structure — Bangkok's temples, markets
and street food, then the Andaman beaches (Phuket / Krabi / Phi Phi).
Family-friendly and honeymoon-friendly at once.

## Best time

- **November–February** for Bangkok (avg ~27°C days); **November–March** for
  Phuket/Krabi/Phi Phi. April is the hottest month (Songkran water festival
  mid-April); Andaman monsoon roughly May–Oct.

## Visa (checkable, for Indian travellers — researched 2026)

- **No visa needed** for tourism up to 60 days (extendable by 30); must
  complete the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online within 72 h
  before departure. Re-verify at booking; rules changed recently and can
  change again.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew, Bangkok** — royal complex from 1782
   housing the Emerald Buddha, carved from a single block of jade/jasper;
   strict dress code.
2. **Wat Arun** — the 'Temple of Dawn' on the Chao Phraya, its central
   prang decorated with porcelain shards; classic sunset river shot.
3. **Wat Pho** — home of the 46 m gold-plated Reclining Buddha and the
   traditional massage school.
4. **Chatuchak Weekend Market** — one of the world's largest weekend
   markets, ~15,000 stalls.
5. **Phi Phi Islands / Maya Bay** — limestone-karst archipelago; Maya Bay
   famously closed 2018–2022 for reef recovery, now open with visitor rules
   (no swimming in the bay, capped numbers).
6. **Phang Nga Bay / 'James Bond Island'** — Khao Phing Kan's needle rock
   from The Man with the Golden Gun (1974); sea-canoe caves.
7. **Railay, Krabi** — beach peninsula cut off by cliffs, reachable only by
   longtail boat; world-class rock climbing.

## Activities shortlist

- Longtail-boat temple day on the Chao Phraya (Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun)
- Bangkok street-food night walk (pad thai, mango sticky rice, boat noodles)
- Chatuchak weekend-market treasure hunt
- Floating market + railway market combo day trip
- Island-hopping speedboat day: Phi Phi / Maya Bay viewpoint rules-compliant
- Sea-canoeing the caves of Phang Nga Bay
- Longtail to Railay for beaches under the cliffs
- Thai massage / cooking-class afternoon
- Elephant sanctuary visit — ethical, observation-based (no riding)

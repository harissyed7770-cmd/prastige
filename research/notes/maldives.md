## Positioning

The honeymoon crown jewel — and easier than its reputation: Indians get a
free 30-day visa on arrival, and short flights connect Indian metros to
Malé. Two distinct products to explain honestly: private-island resorts
(one island = one resort, overwater villas, seaplane arrivals) vs the newer
guesthouse/local-island economy at gentler budgets.

## Best time

- **November–April**: the dry high season — calm seas, most sun.
- **June–October** (wet season): bioluminescent plankton blooms peak;
  brightest on new-moon nights. Lower rates in low season.

## Visa (checkable, for Indian travellers — researched 2026)

- Free visa on arrival, 30 days, no advance application; extendable (paid)
  up to 90 days total. Re-verify at booking.

## Attraction/experience facts (checkable)

1. **Overwater villas** — the signature stay: stilted villas over the
   lagoon, many with glass floor panels and direct ladder access to the
   water.
2. **North Malé (Kaafu) Atoll** — home of Malé and Velana International
   Airport; the most accessible atoll with some of the country's most
   established reefs and resorts.
3. **Bioluminescence ('Sea of Stars')** — dinoflagellate plankton that
   glow when disturbed; associated famously with Vaadhoo Island; most
   likely June–October on dark new-moon nights.
4. **Snorkelling house reefs** — many islands have reef ledges metres from
   the beach; mantas and whale sharks congregate in certain atolls
   (Baa Atoll's Hanifaru Bay is UNESCO-biosphere famous, seasonal).
5. **Malé city** — one of the world's most densely populated small
   islands (~133,000 people); fish market, Old Friday Mosque of carved
   coral stone (1658).
6. **Sandbank picnics & dolphin cruises** — classic excursions: private
   sandbank drop-offs, sunset dolphin-spotting dhoni cruises.

## Activities shortlist

- Overwater-villa stay with lagoon ladder access
- House-reef snorkelling straight off the beach
- Sunset dolphin cruise by traditional dhoni
- Sandbank picnic (couples' classic)
- Seaplane transfer as an experience in itself (aerial atoll views)
- Night walk hunting bioluminescence (wet season, new moon)
- Diving day trips (manta/whale-shark seasons by atoll)
- Malé half-day: fish market + coral-stone Old Friday Mosque
- Spa-over-water treatment

## Positioning

India's smallest state but its most famous beach destination — the real story
is the split personality: the busy water-sports beach belt of the north
(Baga–Calangute–Anjuna) vs the quiet palm-fringed south (Palolem, Colva), plus
a 450-year Portuguese heritage layer (Old Goa churches, Fontainhas) that most
beach-only itineraries miss. Family-friendly angle matters — our package
audience is families.

## Best time

- **November–February**: dry, 21–32°C, sea calm and clear — beach + sightseeing
  prime season. January notably less hectic than December peak.
- Monsoon (June–Sept) is the alternate story: Dudhsagar Falls at full flow,
  lush spice farms; Sao Joao festival (24 June). Feb: Goa Carnival.
- Feast of St. Francis Xavier: 3 December (Old Goa, at Basilica of Bom Jesus).

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Basilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa** — consecrated 1605; part of the UNESCO
   World Heritage "Churches and Convents of Goa"; holds the relics of
   St. Francis Xavier.
2. **Dudhsagar Falls** — four-tiered waterfall on the Mandovi River, ~310 m
   drop, inside Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary; reached by shared jeep safari from
   Kulem; fullest during/after monsoon.
3. **Fontainhas, Panaji** — Portuguese-era Latin Quarter; narrow lanes of
   ochre-yellow, green and blue heritage houses; azulejo tile name-plates.
4. **Fort Aguada** — 1612 Portuguese sea fort above Sinquerim Beach with a
   four-storey lighthouse (one of Asia's oldest of its type).
5. **Anjuna Flea Market** — Wednesday market running since the 1960s
   hippie era; north-Goa institution.
6. **Baga & Calangute beaches** — the state's water-sports hub: parasailing,
   jet-ski, banana boat; contiguous ~7 km stretch with Candolim.

## Activities shortlist

- Water sports at Baga–Calangute (parasailing, jet-ski, banana boat)
- Dudhsagar Falls jeep safari + swim in the plunge pool (seasonal)
- Heritage walk through Fontainhas' painted lanes
- Old Goa churches circuit (Bom Jesus + Se Cathedral)
- Sunset cruise on the Mandovi River from Panaji
- Spice plantation tour in Ponda (cardamom, pepper, vanilla) with Goan lunch
- Beach-shack seafood crawl (recheado, xacuti, bebinca for dessert)
- Saturday Night Market at Arpora / Wednesday flea market at Anjuna

## Sample itinerary (5 days — matches Goa Family Holiday 4N/5D)

D1 arrive, check-in, sunset at Calangute. D2 North Goa: Fort Aguada,
Baga water sports, Anjuna. D3 Dudhsagar jeep safari + Ponda spice farm lunch.
D4 Old Goa churches, Fontainhas walk, Mandovi sunset cruise. D5 depart.

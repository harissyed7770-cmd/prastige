# Research Notes — Switzerland

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (jungfrau.ch, rail-tour guides) +
established facts.

## Positioning

The honeymoon classic for Indian travellers: alpine scenery reached by
famously punctual trains — no trekking required. Anchor experiences: the
Jungfrau Railway to Europe's highest station, panoramic trains (Glacier
Express / GoldenPass), Interlaken between its two lakes, Lucerne's medieval
old town, and the Montreux end of Lake Geneva with Chillon Castle. Package
audience: couples (premium tier).

## Best time

- **Late June–early September**: everything open (all lifts, high trails),
  wildflower meadows — peak crowds. **Mid Sept–Oct**: quieter, crisp, still
  open. **Dec–Mar**: snow-season alternative. Jungfraujoch itself is snowy
  year-round.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Jungfraujoch — "Top of Europe"** — Europe's highest railway station at
   3,454 m; reached from Interlaken Ost via Kleine Scheidegg (or the Eiger
   Express cable car); Ice Palace tunnels and Sphinx terrace overlook the
   Aletsch Glacier, the Alps' longest.
2. **Glacier Express** — panoramic train billed as the world's slowest
   express: ~8 h Zermatt→St. Moritz, 291 bridges and 91 tunnels, crossing
   the 2,033 m Oberalp Pass.
3. **Interlaken** — resort town wedged between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz;
   base for Schilthorn/Jungfrau excursions; Harder Kulm funicular viewpoint.
4. **Lucerne** — medieval old town on its lake; the wooden Chapel Bridge
   (Kapellbrücke, 14th c.) is among Europe's oldest covered wooden bridges;
   Mt. Pilatus/Rigi day trips by boat + cogwheel.
5. **Lake Geneva / Montreux** — lakeside promenade; Chillon Castle
   (Château de Chillon), a moated 12th-c. island castle, is one of
   Switzerland's most-visited historic buildings.
6. **GoldenPass Express** — panoramic route Interlaken→Montreux linking the
   Bernese Oberland to the Riviera vaudoise.

## Activities shortlist

- Jungfrau Railway day to 3,454 m (Ice Palace + Sphinx terrace)
- Glacier Express panoramic segment (e.g. Andermatt→Chur) or full run
- Lake Brienz/Thun boat cruise
- Harder Kulm sunset funicular above Interlaken
- Chapel Bridge + old-town walk, Lucerne; Mt. Pilatus golden round trip
- Chillon Castle visit from Montreux
- Swiss chocolate/cheese tasting experiences (no brand partnerships claimed)
- Paragliding over Interlaken (optional adrenaline add-on)

# Research Notes — Kerala

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (best-time guides, backwater/houseboat
guides) + established geographic facts.

## Positioning

The classic South Indian circuit and a natural flagship for a Bangalore-based
agency: hill-station tea country (Munnar), wildlife (Periyar), colonial history
(Fort Kochi) and the thing nowhere else in India has — overnight kettuvallam
houseboats on the Vembanad backwaters. Our package audience is couples
(luxury tier), so romance + Ayurveda angles lead.

## Best time

- **October–March**: 18–30°C, post-monsoon lushness with reliable cruising
  weather on the backwaters; late Nov–mid Dec is a sweet spot.
- Monsoon (June–Sept) is traditional Ayurveda season — therapies are
  considered most effective in the rains; landscapes at their greenest.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Alleppey backwaters** — network of canals, lagoons and paddy-field
   villages around Vembanad Lake; converted rice-barge houseboats
   (kettuvallams) cruise it overnight.
2. **Munnar** — tea town at roughly 1,450–1,600 m in the Western Ghats,
   surrounded by plantation carpet; nearby Eravikulam National Park protects
   the endangered Nilgiri tahr; Anamudi (2,695 m) is peninsular India's
   highest peak.
3. **Periyar (Thekkady)** — tiger reserve in the Western Ghats where safaris
   happen by boat on Periyar Lake; surrounded by cardamom and pepper country.
4. **Fort Kochi** — 16th-century European trading settlement; St. Francis
   Church (1503) is India's oldest European church and briefly held Vasco da
   Gama's remains; shore-operated Chinese fishing nets line the harbour.
5. **Kathakali** — classical dance-drama of Kerala; Kochi cultural centres
   stage nightly performances with make-up demonstrations beforehand.
6. **Athirappilly Falls** — ~24 m wide-curtain falls on the Chalakudy River,
   often called the largest waterfall in Kerala.

## Activities shortlist

- Overnight kettuvallam houseboat cruise with onboard Kerala meals
- Tea-estate walk + tea museum visit in Munnar

- Ayurvedic couples massage / wellness session
- Sunset at the Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi
- Village shikara (canoe) ride through the narrow Alleppey canals
- Kerala sadya — banana-leaf feast

## Sample itinerary (6 days — matches Kerala Backwaters Tour 5N/6D)

D1 arrive Kochi, Fort Kochi walk + Kathakali. D2 drive to Munnar (tea country).
D3 Munnar: Eravikulam NP, tea museum. D4 Thekkady: Periyar boat safari, spice
garden. D5 Alleppey: board houseboat, overnight on the backwaters.
D6 disembark, depart Kochi.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Abu Dhabi** — among the world's largest
   mosques: 82 domes, 1,000+ columns, 24-carat gold-gilded chandeliers and
   the world's largest hand-knotted carpet; free guided tours; modest dress
   required; main hall closed to visitors Fri ~12–3 pm; glows at dusk.
2. **Louvre Abu Dhabi** — on Saadiyat Island; Jean Nouvel's "rain of light"
   dome; the first universal museum of its kind in the Arab world (opened
   2017).
3. **Qasr Al Watan** — the working Presidential Palace in Abu Dhabi, opened
   to visitors in 2019; great hall and library tours.
4. **Yas Island** — Ferrari World (home of Formula Rossa, among the world's
   fastest roller coasters), Warner Bros. World, Yas Waterworld and the F1
   circuit.
5. **Sharjah's museum quarter** — Museum of Islamic Civilization, Sharjah
   Art Museum and a dense heritage district; Sharjah was UNESCO's Cultural
   Capital of the Arab World (1998).
6. **Fujairah & the east coast** — Hajar mountains, Snoopy Island
   snorkelling, and Al-Bidyah, one of the UAE's oldest mosques.

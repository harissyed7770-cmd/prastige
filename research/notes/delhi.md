# Research Notes — Delhi

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (monument guides, best-time guides) +
established heritage facts.

## Positioning

Two cities in one: Mughal Old Delhi (Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Chandni Chowk food
lanes) and Lutyens' New Delhi (India Gate, Rajpath axis), with three UNESCO
World Heritage monuments inside city limits. Works as a standalone city break
or the gateway to the Golden Triangle (Agra/Jaipur add-ons).

## Best time

- **October–March**: 15–25°C sightseeing weather; festival season (Diwali).
  Avoid Apr–Jun heat; Jul–Sept is monsoon. (Dec–Jan can bring fog/smog —
  keep mornings flexible.)

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Red Fort** — built by Shah Jahan from 1639; UNESCO site; red-sandstone
   walls run over 2 km; seat of Mughal power for ~200 years; PM's
   Independence Day address happens from its ramparts.
2. **Qutub Minar** — 72.5 m, the world's tallest brick minaret; begun 1199
   under Qutb ud-Din Aibak; UNESCO site; the rust-resistant Iron Pillar
   (4th c.) stands in its courtyard.
3. **Humayun's Tomb** — commissioned 1565 by Empress Bega Begum; UNESCO
   site; first grand charbagh (four-part garden) tomb in India and the
   acknowledged design precursor of the Taj Mahal.
4. **Jama Masjid** — Shah Jahan's great congregational mosque (1656); one of
   India's largest, courtyard holding many thousands of worshippers.
5. **India Gate & Kartavya Path** — WWI memorial arch by Lutyens on the
   ceremonial axis; evening picnic institution.
6. **Chandni Chowk** — 17th-century market street of Old Delhi; paratha and
   jalebi lanes, spice market at Khari Baoli (among Asia's largest).

## Activities shortlist

- Cycle-rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk's lanes
- Old Delhi street-food walk (parathas, jalebis, daulat ki chaat in winter)
- Sound-and-light / evening walk at Red Fort
- Sunset lawns at India Gate
- Lodhi Garden morning walk among 15th-century tombs
- Akshardham or Lotus Temple visit
- Dilli Haat crafts-and-regional-food bazaar
- Day-trip add-on: Taj Mahal at Agra (~3–4 h by expressway/train)

# Research Notes — Rajasthan

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (Rajasthan Tourism, fort guides) +
established heritage facts.

## Positioning

India's headline heritage circuit: three distinctly coloured cities — pink
Jaipur, blue Jodhpur, white Udaipur — each anchored by a fort or palace of
genuine world rank, plus the Thar desert as an add-on. Package audience is
"All" (premium tier), so pitch spans couples, families and culture-first
travellers.

## Best time

- **October–March**: dry and cool; summers (esp. May) are oppressively hot in
  the desert belt. Visit forts first thing in the morning — Amber crowds
  build from ~10 am.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Amber (Amer) Fort, Jaipur** — begun 1592 under Raja Man Singh I, red
   sandstone and marble; capital of the Kachwaha Rajputs before Jaipur; the
   Sheesh Mahal's mirror mosaics are the showpiece. Open from 8 am; allow
   2–3 hours.
2. **Hawa Mahal, Jaipur** — 1799 "Palace of Winds"; five-storey pink facade
   with 953 jharokha windows built so royal women could watch street life
   unseen.
3. **Jaipur walled city** — UNESCO World Heritage listed (2019) as a planned
   grid city of 1727.
4. **Mehrangarh Fort, Jodhpur** — founded c. 1459 by Rao Jodha; ramparts rise
   about 120 m over the old city, whose indigo-washed houses give Jodhpur the
   "Blue City" name.
5. **City Palace, Udaipur** — begun 1553 under Udai Singh II; Rajasthan's
   largest palace complex, terraced above Lake Pichola with the Lake Palace
   seemingly afloat mid-lake.
6. **Jaisalmer Fort** — built 1156; one of the world's few "living forts",
   with a quarter of the old city's population still resident inside its
   yellow-sandstone walls.

## Activities shortlist

- Sunrise walk through Amber Fort before the crowds
- Old-city bazaar crawl in Jaipur (block-print textiles, blue pottery, gems)
- Boat ride on Lake Pichola at sunset
- Zip-line or rampart walk at Mehrangarh; Blue City rooftop views
- Camel safari + dunes sunset at Sam (Jaisalmer add-on)
- Rajasthani thali + folk performance evening (kalbeliya dance, puppetry)
- Stepwell stop: Panna Meena ka Kund near Amber
- Heritage-haveli stay experience

## Sample itinerary (7 days — matches Rajasthan Heritage Tour 6N/7D)

D1 arrive Jaipur, old city + Hawa Mahal facade. D2 Amber Fort, City Palace,
bazaars. D3 drive/fly to Jodhpur, clock-tower market. D4 Mehrangarh + Blue
City walk, drive toward Udaipur (via Ranakpur option). D5 Udaipur City Palace.
D6 Lake Pichola boat + old-town lanes. D7 depart.

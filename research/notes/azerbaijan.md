# Research Notes — Azerbaijan

Researched 2026-07-29. Sources: web search (Baku guides, evisa.gov.az-linked
guides, season guides) + established facts.

## Positioning

The "budget Europe feel" break trending with Indian travellers: Baku's mix of
UNESCO-listed medieval Old City and futuristic skyline (Flame Towers, Zaha
Hadid's Heydar Aliyev Center), plus genuinely weird nature an hour away —
40,000-year-old petroglyphs, half the world's mud volcanoes, and a hillside
that has burned continuously for decades. Short flights, easy e-visa.

## Best time

- **April–June and September–October**: 15–25°C, ideal for the city's
  boulevards and day trips. July–Aug tops 30°C; winter is windy ("city of
  winds").

## Visa (checkable, for Indian travellers — researched 2026)

- ASAN e-visa: single-entry ~USD 29 (~₹2,450), 30-day stay, valid 90 days
  from issue, standard processing 3 working days (3-hour urgent option).
  Re-verify at booking.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Icherisheher (Old City), Baku** — UNESCO World Heritage; walled
   medieval core holding the 12th-century Maiden Tower and the
   15th-century Palace of the Shirvanshahs.
2. **Flame Towers** — trio of flame-shaped towers up to 182 m; LED facades
   ripple with fire animations after dark; best seen from the Caspian
   boulevard or the funicular viewpoint.
3. **Heydar Aliyev Center** — Zaha Hadid's wave-form cultural centre
   (opened 2012); an architecture-pilgrimage building.
4. **Gobustan National Park** — UNESCO-listed; ~6,000 petroglyphs up to
   40,000 years old, about 65 km from Baku.
5. **Mud volcanoes** — Azerbaijan hosts nearly half the world's mud
   volcanoes; cold, gurgling grey cones near Gobustan.
6. **Yanar Dag** — "Burning Mountain": a natural-gas hillside flame that
   has burned continuously for decades on the Absheron Peninsula.
7. **Ateshgah Fire Temple** — 17th–18th-century temple complex at Surakhani
   with Indian (Hindu/Sikh) merchant inscriptions — a genuine India
   connection to tell.

## Activities shortlist

- Old City walk: Maiden Tower, Shirvanshahs' Palace, carpet shops
- Funicular to Highland Park for Flame Towers + bay panorama
- Gobustan petroglyphs + mud volcano jeep combo day
- Yanar Dag + Ateshgah Absheron half-day
- Caspian boulevard evening (Baku Eye, tea houses)
- Heydar Aliyev Center photo stop + interior visit
- Azerbaijani food crawl: plov, dolma, qutab, pomegranate everything
- Day trip to Gabala/Shahdag mountains (seasonal add-on)

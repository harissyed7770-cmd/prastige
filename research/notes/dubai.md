## Positioning

The superlatives city, four hours from India: world's tallest building,
one of the world's biggest malls, man-made palm islands — balanced against
old Dubai (creek dhows, gold and spice souks) and the desert an hour out.
Package audience: couples, luxury tier. Direct flights from Bengaluru.

## Best time

- **November–April**: mild 20s–low 30s °C, outdoor-friendly; summer
  (Jun–Sept) regularly exceeds 40°C — indoor attractions still work then.

## Attraction facts (checkable)

1. **Burj Khalifa** — world's tallest building at 828 m (829.8 m to tip);
   "At the Top" observation decks on level 124/125, with a higher lounge at
   148; the Dubai Fountain performs at its feet.
2. **Dubai Mall** — among the world's largest malls: 1,200+ stores, a
   walk-through aquarium tunnel and an Olympic-size ice rink.
3. **Desert safari** — dune-bashing in 4x4s (or ATV add-ons), camel rides,
   sunset photo stop, BBQ dinner with live shows at a Bedouin-style camp.
4. **Palm Jumeirah** — man-made palm-shaped island; The View at The Palm /
   Atlantis at the crown; monorail spine.
5. **Old Dubai** — abra (wooden ferry) across Dubai Creek for about AED 1;
   Gold Souk and Spice Souk in Deira; Al Fahidi heritage quarter.
6. **Dubai Frame** — 150 m picture-frame structure with old-Dubai/new-Dubai
   views either side; **Museum of the Future** — the torus-shaped landmark
   on Sheikh Zayed Road (opened 2022).
7. **Dubai Marina** — dhow and yacht cruises through the skyscraper canyon.

## Activities shortlist

- At the Top (level 124/125) timed for sunset + Dubai Fountain show after
- Desert safari evening (dune bashing, camel ride, BBQ camp)
- Abra ride + Gold/Spice Souk wander in Deira
- Dubai Marina dinner cruise
- Beach day at JBR / Kite Beach with skyline backdrop
- Dubai Mall + aquarium; Mall of the Emirates (Ski Dubai option)
- Palm Jumeirah viewpoint + monorail
- Global Village / Miracle Garden (seasonal, roughly Nov–Apr)

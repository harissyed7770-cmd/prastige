## Candidate additions — OPTIONS ONLY, not in the working list

Flagged for owner decision; none of these get content unless approved.

### Domestic candidates

- **Himachal Pradesh** (`himachal`) — Shimla/Manali is a staple of
  Bangalore-market honeymoon and family itineraries; obvious catalog gap.
- **Andaman Islands** (`andaman`) — domestic beach alternative to Maldives at
  a lower price point; pairs well with the family/couples audience.
- **Coorg** (`coorg`) — weekend-drive distance from Bengaluru (the agency's
  home market); low-cost short-break inventory.

### International candidates

- **Bali** (`bali`) — one of the highest-demand honeymoon destinations for
  Indian travellers; visa-on-arrival keeps friction low.
- **Vietnam** (`vietnam`) — fast-growing budget-international favourite from
  India with direct flights from Indian metros.
- **Sri Lanka** (`sri-lanka`) — short-haul, visa-easy, works as an
  entry-level international package.

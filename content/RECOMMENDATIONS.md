# Recommendations — Closing the Known Gaps

Based on the reference crawl (known gaps) and the competitor scan
(research/notes/competitor-scan.md). Prioritized: P1 = ship with the new
site, P2 = ship soon after with owner input, P3 = later.

## P1 — Build into the new site now (no owner input needed)

1. **Fix the broken destination links.** Maldives and Dubai pages are now
   written (`researched_complete`) — the new site ships all 14 with zero
   404s. *(Gap: broken footer links.)*
2. **Day-by-day itineraries on every package.** All 7 packages now carry
   full itineraries; render them on the page, not behind an enquiry. Every
   credible competitor shows the plan up front. *(Gap: packages were
   cards-only.)*
3. **De-templatize destination pages.** New content has 5–6 attractions
   with checkable specifics, place-specific activities, and a real
   best-time-with-reason — vs the old site's identical 2-attraction
   template. *(Gap: thin destination pages.)*
4. **Kill the unverifiable stats strip.** No "1000+ / 50+ / 5+ years"
   anywhere. The about copy now works without numbers. *(Gap: unverifiable
   claims — and "50+ destinations" contradicted the visible 14.)*
5. **Keep and upgrade the WhatsApp pattern.** The pre-filled enquiry
   template was the old site's best feature; every package now has a
   package-specific prefill (name + duration baked in), so enquiries arrive
   pre-qualified.
6. **Consistent card anatomy.** One fixed field order on every package card
   (name, duration, tier, audience, highlights, CTA) — consistency itself
   reads as credibility (Thrillophilia pattern).

## P2 — Needs owner input, high value (collect within weeks of launch)

7. **Real pricing anchors.** Every credible competitor shows at least
   "From ₹X per person". Recommend: indicative "from" prices per package
   with a "final quote on WhatsApp" note. **Blocker: owner must supply
   floor prices.** *(Gap: no pricing anywhere.)*
8. **Real testimonials.** The design ships with a testimonial slot built
   but hidden. Recommend the Pickyourtrail format: guest first name + city
   + trip taken + a sentence naming who helped them. Collect via a
   post-trip WhatsApp message. **Never fabricate; slot stays hidden until
   ≥3 real quotes exist.** *(Gap: zero social proof.)*
9. **Truthful founding-year line.** Footer said "since years" (broken).
   One real number from the owner fixes it ("Planning holidays from
   Bengaluru since 20XX"). *(Gap: broken copy + no era anchor.)*
10. **Real photography.** Per-destination shot lists are ready in
    /research/notes/*.md. Until real/licensed photos exist, use restrained
    licensed stock — never watermarked or obviously-AI imagery. *(Gap: no
    photo evidence.)*

## P3 — Later, nice-to-have

11. **Named humans.** "Your trip is planned by <name>" (owner/staff) with a
    photo — the strongest small-agency trust signal seen in the scan
    (Veena World's named tour managers). Needs owner comfort.
12. **Certifications strip, if real.** IATA/TAAI/TAFI badges only if the
    business actually holds memberships — ask; never decorative.
13. **Per-package guest counts** once real bookings accumulate ("12
    families took this trip in 2026") — small true numbers beat big vague
    ones.
14. **Beef up /customize-trip** into a short guided form mirroring the
    WhatsApp template fields (persona → dates → budget band → hotel tier),
    Pickyourtrail-style. *(Gap: customize-trip page was thin.)*

## Explicitly rejected

- Live-booking urgency tickers and inflated counters — dishonest at this
  scale, and the exact pattern that broke trust on the old site.
- Marketplace-style breadth claims. Position: **14 destinations, planned
  deeply, by people you can call.**

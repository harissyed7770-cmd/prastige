Rejected during sourcing: an initial "Burj Khalifa" pick showed only an
observation-deck viewing telescope, not the tower; an initial "Dubai Mall"
pick showed an unrelated interior corridor with a red phone-booth novelty;
an initial "Dubai Frame" pick showed the interior observation walkway
rather than the recognizable golden frame exterior — all three swapped for
verified matches.
